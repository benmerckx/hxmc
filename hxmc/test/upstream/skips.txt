# The tests of upstream's runtime suites that UpstreamSuites.hx leaves out on
# hxmc, one per line:
#   <suite> <native|wasm|*> <class>[.<method>] <category> <reason>
# BUG: an hxmc bug or missing feature, not fixed yet (docs/testing.md lists them);
# TARGET: a behaviour hxmc chooses (that of hl, cpp and jvm where it can);
# EXTERNAL: a service, a tool or another target's natives the test needs (as
# upstream's CI leaves them out where they are missing).
# A whole class that the suite finds by its directory is removed from the copy;
# every other entry is left out by utest's UTEST_PATTERN (the whole method).
